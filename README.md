# Automobile Dashboard Analysis | POWER BI #

## Objective ##
The primary objective of the analysis is to gain insights into the sales performance of different car brands across various countries, track sales trends over time, and identify patterns and relationships in the dataset. This can help in understanding customer preferences, optimizing inventory, and improving sales strategies.

## Key Analysis ##
•	Total sales for each year using DAX\
•	Measure to calculate the average sales per month.\
•	Cumulative total of sales over months\
•	DAX formula to calculate the Year-to-Date (YTD) sales.\
•	The percentage growth in sales compared to the previous year\
•	Measure to calculate the total sales for the last 12 months.\
•	Calculate the highest sales month using DAX

## Key Insights ##
•	Identifies monthly/yearly sales fluctuations and peak sales periods to forecast future demand.\
•	Analyzes top-performing vehicle types and models, highlighting popular specifications.\
•	Measures sales performance differences between recession periods and non-recession periods.\
•	Visualizes sales performance by geographical location or dealership using interactive maps.\
•	Tracks essential metrics like total units sold, total revenue, and average price per unit.\
•	Evaluates profitability based on price variation, fuel type, transmission, and vehicle engine size.\
•	Links advertising expenditure to sales revenue to measure marketing ROI
